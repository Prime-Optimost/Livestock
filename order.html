How to order

There is no online checkout. You send a request, we send a price, and you confirm. Here is what happens at each step.

Three steps, start to finish

  1. Send a requestTell us the product, the number you want and the date you need it. Use the quote form, WhatsApp, a phone call or the printable form.
  2. Get your quoteWe reply by phone or WhatsApp with a price. Prices are quoted per order and depend on market rates, animal weights and the season.
  3. Confirm and payOnce you agree the price, confirm the order. Pay by mobile money or bank transfer, on delivery or once the weight is confirmed.

What to send us

The more you tell us up front, the faster we can come back with a price.

Your order

  • The product: live goats, live sheep, breeding stock or manure
  • How many you want, or roughly how many
  • The date you need them, especially for festivals and functions
  • Whether you will collect at the ranch or need delivery

Your details

  • Your name
  • A phone number or WhatsApp number we can reach you on
  • Your town or region
  • Anything else we should know, such as the occasion or the size you are looking for

Paying and collecting

  • Payment is by mobile money (MTN, Telecel, AT) or bank transfer.
  • You pay on delivery, or once the animal’s weight is confirmed.
  • Collection is at the ranch in Santasi, Kumasi.
  • Delivery can be arranged. Ask when you send your request.

Live animals follow the ranch’s production and breeding cycles, so ask early. Manure is available all year round.
