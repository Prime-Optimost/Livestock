What we sell

Live goats, live sheep, breeding stock and organic manure. Prices are quoted per order, so tell us what you need and we’ll come back with a price.

Product 01

Live goats

Finisher goats, sold live. They are raised on the ranch under a semi-intensive system, in raised housing with slatted floors, and they are sold straight to households, traders, butchers and caterers.

  • Sold live, by the animal
  • Weights and prices confirmed before you pay
  • Collection at the ranch, or delivery by arrangement
Request a quote for goats
Product 02

Live sheep

Finisher sheep, sold live, raised the same way as the goats. Popular for family occasions, festivals and catering, and available to traders and bulk buyers as well as households.

  • Sold live, by the animal
  • Good for festive and catering orders, so order ahead
  • Collection at the ranch, or delivery by arrangement
Request a quote for sheep
Product 03

Breeding stock

Goats and sheep for starting or growing your own herd. Tell us the type and number you are looking for and we will tell you what is available from the current stock.

  • For new and expanding herds
  • Availability depends on the breeding cycle
  • Talk to us early so we can plan the order

Product 04

Organic manure

Manure collected from beneath the slatted housing floors, for farms, gardens and landscaping. It is available all year round, unlike the live animals.

  • Available year-round
  • Bagged or loose, by arrangement
  • Suitable for crop farms, vegetable gardens and lawns
Request a quote for manure

How prices work

  • Prices are quoted per order, based on current market rates, animal weights and the season.
  • Live animals depend on the ranch’s production and breeding cycles, so ask before you plan.
  • Manure is available year-round.
  • Payment is by mobile money (MTN, Telecel, AT) or bank transfer, on delivery or once weights are confirmed.

Need more than one product? Say so in the notes on your request and we will quote for the whole order.
